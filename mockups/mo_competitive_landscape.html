Mo / Aevah — Differentiation & Moat

Seven durable advantages — not features, but structural positions that compound as the platform matures and data accumulates.

01
Causal ML, not BI
Mo explains why demand changed — causal event detection, cannibalization DAGs, pre/post methodology. Competitors show dashboards. Mo surfaces decisions with evidence trails. This is the difference between a reporting tool and an analytical co-pilot.
Event directional accuracy: 63% on 30,876 validated events
02
TDP as a First-Class Feature
Distribution velocity (TDP) is the single strongest predictor of CPG demand — yet no other mid-market platform incorporates it into forecasts. This domain knowledge is the primary driver of Mo's 4% wMAPE vs. 30–118% for off-the-shelf models.
MO_62: Aevah 6.1% vs. foundation model avg 31.5% (5.1× gap)
03
Cannibalization Scoring at SKU × Retailer
Real-time cannibalization probability for every own-brand pair, at every retailer, every week. Competitors don't offer this for mid-market CPG. BUILT uses it to know — before a buyer conversation — whether launching a new SKU grows the category or steals from itself.
60,695 scored pairs across 78 retailers; cannibal_prob ≥ 0.99 for confirmed pairs
04
Price Point vs. Intelligence Depth
SymphonyAI starts at $500K/year. NIQ contracts run $100K–$500K+. Mo delivers enterprise-grade causal intelligence at mid-market pricing — serving the brands NIQ wasn't designed for and Symphony can't justify deploying to. The addressable market is enormous.
Target: brands outgrowing Byzzer ($199/mo reports) but not ready for $500K SymphonyAI
05
Speed: Weeks, Not Quarters
Enterprise CPG analytics implementations take 6–18 months. Mo goes live in 2–4 weeks from SPINS data to live forecast, because the pipeline is built specifically for the SPINS data model. Brands can make decisions before competitors have even finished onboarding elsewhere.
Full ML pipeline (P0–P12): data → scored Druid tables in one run
06
LLM-Native from the Ground Up
Mo Chat is grounded in live Druid data per screen — not a chatbot bolted onto a BI tool. Provider-agnostic (GPT-4o, Claude, Qwen, Azure, open-source). Competitors are adding AI as a feature; Mo was architected as an AI-first decision surface from day one.
14 screen-aware proactive messages; streaming SSE; <5s response target
07
Aevah as an Open Harness
Scored outputs — cannibalization rates, elasticities, promo lift, forecasts — are published back to Druid as API endpoints. Client data science teams can consume them directly. This is the opposite of a walled garden: Aevah becomes infrastructure, not a subscription to replace.
All scores served as Druid tables: scored_cannibalization, scored_price_elasticity, cannibalization_rate_forecast_weekly, …
