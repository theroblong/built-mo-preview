Caveats & Limitations

Known constraints before integrating CRX into Mo. Address before production use.

No competitor data in CRX
CRX is a brand-specific feed — BUILT products only. Cross-brand cannibalization and share-of-shelf analysis are impossible at Costco via CRX. Costco competitive benchmarks must come from SPINS CLUB channel (if subscribed).
✅ UPC crosswalk confirmed
docs/Costco Item Table.csv received 2026-09-15. 9 BUILT Costco items, all UPCs confirmed (all 840229XXXXXX prefix). Join key: Item column matches BP222 exactly. Pack counts: 13 bars (original bar), 14 bars (all Puffs).
1 CRX unit ≠ 1 bar
1 CRX unit = 1 Club Pack = 16 or 18 BUILT Bars. Never compare unit_sales to SPINS base_units without applying pack multiplier. Use dpwpw (dollar-based) as the primary Costco velocity metric to avoid unit confusion.
Inventory multiples are pallets, not demand
on_hand, on_order, qty_received appear in multiples of 525 — Costco's pallet unit count. These reflect ordering behavior, not consumer sell-through. Do not use for demand planning without conversion.
OOS / In Stock% unusable
oos and in_stock_pct are ~100% null in BP222 (5.4M rows). Circana is investigating. Do not surface these fields in Mo until confirmed usable. Suppress or show "N/A".
Dollar fields are strings — must parse
All currency columns in BP222 arrive as formatted strings: "$2,258.87" and "($19.99)" for negatives. MO_74 handles this. If loading BP222 directly (bypassing MO_74), every money column will be null or wrong.
BP222 refresh cadence TBD
Current file covers Jan 2023–Aug 2026. How and when Circana delivers updated extracts to MinIO has not been confirmed with Justin. Pipeline can be built but the update trigger is unknown.
No MVM baseline in CRX
CRX has no built-in Base/Incremental split. MVM lift proxy = compare MVM-week unit_sales vs. prior-period or year-ago. is_mvm flag (coupon_units > 0) marks the event weeks. TPRs and COMPs are not captured in CRX promo measures.
