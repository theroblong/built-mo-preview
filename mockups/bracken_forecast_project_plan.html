Bracken's core concern — June 26
"3 years of data but it's not comparable data. Promotional lift in '25 won't be what it is in '26 and '27."
The model is built to handle this. It separates base demand from promo-driven volume at every SKU and retailer, tracks year-ago lift rates as a signal, and retrains quarterly so each forecast reflects the current promotional regime — not a historical average. The Oct 2025 backtest (29-week out-of-sample) showed 6.1% error during BUILT's highest-growth phase precisely because the model adapts.
