Spotlight: PB Cup 4pk — Meijer vs. Walmart

At Walmart (Mass Merch RMA), PB Cup shows a 47% cross-brand cannibal rate — taking significant share from competitors. At Meijer, that rate is only 12.5%, with 42 of 48 competitor pairs scoring as Incremental. This means PB Cup is generating genuinely new demand at Meijer — customers buying it who would not have bought a competing bar — making it a category-building SKU at this account specifically.

Reading the scores: The cannibal model probability (99.9x%) reflects structural flavor competition — it is high because these pairings are inherently competitive product substitutes. "Low confidence" is a separate data-maturity flag tracking how many weeks of Meijer FOOD RMA data have accumulated. All 5 core SKUs are still below Mo's 20-week full-confidence threshold. The model direction (cannibalizing vs. incremental) will not change materially as more weeks accumulate, but the confidence label will upgrade.
Key takeaway: Coconut 4pk is BUILT's top competitive weapon at Meijer: 84.6% cross-brand displacement rate, drawing from 11 distinct competitor brands with no own-brand friction. PB Cup tells the opposite and more powerful story — it is a category builder at Meijer, with only 12.5% cross-brand displacement and 42 of 48 competitive pairs generating new demand. Cookie Dough carries the most internal friction (75% own-brand rate) and warrants the closest assortment attention. Meijer's overall 46.9% rate sits above Kroger (43.0%), consistent with a healthy — not over-competitive — growth story.

Meijer ε = −0.489 (Moderately Elastic)

A 10% price increase at Meijer leads to roughly a 4.9% volume decline — meaningful but not hyper-sensitive. This is within the range where promotional pricing (temporary discounts, BOGOs) generates a measurable lift without permanently anchoring the shopper to a lower price expectation.

Implication for assortment: BUILT's $9.65 ARP sits below Barebells ($10.07) and above Pure Protein / Perfect Bar. There is modest room to increase price without outsized volume loss, but a reduction below $9.00 would generate modest lift.
