Validation & Calibration
Weeks 3–4
Use BUILT's own actuals as the benchmark to verify Mo's accuracy — and to improve it.
  • Forecast backtesting: Mo vs. BUILT's prior-year actuals on held-out retailers
  • Elasticity verification: cross-reference Mo's price signals against known BUILT trade events
  • Distribution accuracy: reconcile SPINS TDP with BUILT's internal distribution records
  • Cannibalization audit: validate Mo's scored pairs against BUILT's portfolio team intuition
  • Coverage gap report: retailers or SKUs thin in SPINS data; agree on plan for each
  • Statistical forecasting enabled for retailer/SKU combinations not yet pipeline-scored
