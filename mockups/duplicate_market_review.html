Duplicate Market Review

Every retailer market pair tested for double-counting, with the evidence for each verdict.

BUILT · SPINS weekly POS · 1 October 2026 · panel through week ending 6 September 2026

What this found

One real duplicate: Circle K. Circle K reports under two SPINS market definitions that cover the same stores — one with Alaska, one without. Both were being counted, so Circle K entered the forecast at roughly twice its true volume.

Giant Eagle and Hy-Vee each also appear under two markets. Both were tested and are genuinely different stores (supermarket banner vs. convenience banner), so both are kept. Six further “with Alaska/Hawaii” markets exist for Albertsons, CVS, Kroger, Sam’s, Target and Walgreens, but all carry zero units and are already excluded.

4. What we would like Brian to confirm

  • Circle K: is CK - CIRCLE K CORP TOTAL W/ ALASKA the market BUILT treats as Circle K total, with CIRCLE K CORP being the same chain excluding Alaska? We propose keeping the with-Alaska market as the more complete figure.
  • Giant Eagle: confirm GIANT EAGLE - GETGO CORP (convenience) and GIANT EAGLE CORP (supermarket) are separate store sets that should both count.
  • Hy-Vee: same question for HY-VEE FAST & FRESH vs HY-VEE CORP.
  • Murphy USA: MURPHY CORP TOTAL is the only market we receive for Murphy. Is anything missing, or is that the complete picture?
  • The six zero-unit markets (Albertsons, CVS, Kroger, Sam’s, Target, Walgreens “with AK/HI”): should these ever carry volume? If they are permanently empty we can exclude them by definition rather than by measurement.

5. Effect if the Circle K duplicate is removed

Portfolio base units move from 61.42M to 60.52M (−1.5%), and series from 2,137 to 2,128. Circle K’s own 13-week forecast drops from 314,246 to about 160,117 units, which is the correction, not a loss.

Nothing is live on this yet — the change is staged pending this review. Because the current models learned from the double-counted history, adopting it means retraining rather than only re-scoring.
